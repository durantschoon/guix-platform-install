========================================================================
oracle/benchmark/ -- workloads for the GIPS substitute benchmark
========================================================================
Protocol: docs/GIPS_BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md.  Runner: oracle/scripts/gips-benchmark.scm.

workload-small.scm
  Four tiny packages.  Purpose: expose per-item overhead (narinfo lookup,
  connection setup, IPFS content routing), which a throughput-dominated
  workload would hide.  If GIPS loses anywhere it is most likely here, which
  is the reason to include it.

workload-medium.scm
  emacs-minimal, git, python.  Purpose: a closure large enough that nar
  transfer dominates, approximating a real first-day install.

STATEMENT OF OMISSION: there is no "large" workload yet.  The natural one is
the local-model runtime from the next goal (see CHECKLIST.md), but whether that
package has central substitutes for the chosen architecture has not been
checked, and a workload the central arm cannot serve fails preflight.

STATEMENT OF OMISSION: manifests name packages, not store paths, on purpose.
The store paths are frozen per experiment by `hub-prepare' into
workload.paths / workload.closure, which belong with the results, not in git:
they are only meaningful for one channel commit and one architecture.
